Things we noticed in your spreadsheet

Nothing has been changed. These are questions for you, not fixes. The desk copies your sheet exactly, including these.

  1. “Profit” is a markup. 50% on cost is a 33% margin; 75% is a 43% margin. Is that what you mean by your 50–75% target?
    Profit row: total cost × profit %
  2. Days are typed in by hand. Square footage only drives tooling and chemicals; crew cost comes from days. How do you decide the days for a job?
  3. Per-sq-ft price includes HST. Customers usually see before-tax prices. The desk shows both.
  4. One formula looks broken. “Angle Grinder Metals” cost per tool averages the segment counts instead of a price ($6.89). Small effect (about $0.003/sq ft).
    Cell C50: =AVERAGE(A39:B57)
  5. Some tooling rates are typed-in 0.02 rather than calculated (30/40 grit, 100 and 200 resin, 400 Reflect). For example 30/40 works out to 0.0215.
  6. Minimal and Burnish carry a “Generator” charge of $0.53/sq ft; Polished doesn't. That's most of why Minimal costs about double per sq ft. Intended?
  7. Monthly costs ÷ 4 to get weekly. A month is about 4.33 weeks, so weekly overhead runs about 8% high. Maybe on purpose as a cushion?
  8. US to Canadian dollars at 1.25 on tooling. Worth checking against today's rate.
  9. Employees at $80/h and $70/h, owner at $53/h. Real numbers, or placeholders?
  10. The “Estimates” tab isn't usable for past jobs: the Warehouse and Shop jobs have identical copied numbers, and the bottom block shows #DIV/0! errors. That's why section 3 asks for your real bids.
